Every organisation, person, fact, and URL below is invented. This fixture shows
the structure and evidence boundaries of a one-subject result, not real research.

## in one line

Juniper Studio says it designs accessible public-service websites for municipal
teams and is paid through fixed-scope projects and support retainers
([organisation site](https://studio.example/about)).

## meeting card

### five things to remember

- It describes accessibility research as part of delivery, not a final audit
  ([organisation site](https://studio.example/about)).
- The public register gives an incorporation year of 2021 and identifies it as
  founder-owned ([register](https://registry.example/companies/juniper-studio)).
- A July update announced a new content-design practice
  ([updates](https://updates.example/juniper/feed.xml)).
- No public revenue or headcount figure was found.
- The independent archive was blocked, so the brief cannot test every company claim.

### three questions to ask

1. How are research findings carried into delivery decisions after discovery?
2. Which responsibilities would sit with each partner on a joint project?
3. What has the new content-design practice changed in active engagements?

### claims not to repeat as facts

- “fast-growing” appears in the organisation's own update, but no independent
  headcount or revenue series was available to verify it

### objective

Decide whether a small joint bid is worth designing; do not agree commercial
terms in the meeting.

## the organisation

The organisation describes municipal digital teams as its buyers and lists
discovery, service design, accessibility research, and ongoing support as its
services ([organisation site](https://studio.example/about)). The Example
Business Register says it was incorporated in 2021 and remains founder-owned
([register](https://registry.example/companies/juniper-studio)). Revenue,
profitability, and current headcount were not published in the approved sources.

## what they say vs what others say

The studio calls itself “fast-growing.” The register confirms that the entity is
active but does not publish growth measures, and the independent archive was
unreadable. Treat growth as an unverified company claim, not a settled fact.

## good to know

The site consistently calls client staff “service owners,” a useful term to
mirror when asking how decisions are shared
([organisation site](https://studio.example/about)).

## open questions

- Is the partnership role commercial only, or involved through delivery?
- What evidence would verify the claimed growth?
