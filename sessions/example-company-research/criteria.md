# criteria — example-company-research

Approved: 2026-08-29
Last amended: —

## must
- explain what Juniper Studio does and who pays for it
- establish age and ownership, or state the public gap
- explain the public responsibilities of the meeting counterpart
- identify material changes in the last twelve months

## nice
- describe how the studio talks about accessibility and research
- identify the clearest alternative a prospective client might compare it with

## open
- “would they be a compatible delivery partner” — judge from published working practices and project descriptions

## notes
- these are questions for one brief, not filters over candidates
- absence of public revenue is a gap, not a negative score
- no prefilter exists because every approved source is read

## not carried over
- private background about employees is outside the published-purpose boundary
